Meet Mr. Ong.

A Deaf taxi driver in Kuala Lumpur, rated 4.9 stars by his passengers.

  1. Assaulted over a miscommunication.
  2. Police cruelly refused him an interpreter.
  3. Intimidated into dropping his report.
  4. July 2026: he sued the police to demand sign language interpreters.

The Deaf have always had a voice.
Society just chose to silence it.
